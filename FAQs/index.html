Fequently Ased Questions

Where is Wedding being held?

The Ceremony and Reception are being held at AutoCamp Yosemite. Please note, the ceremony and receiption will be held outside. We will have umbrellas to provide some shade, but expect it to be warm and sunny!

When should I arrive to the ceremony?

Please plan to arrive at least 15 minutes before the ceremony begins. If you arrive after the ceremony has started, we kindly ask that stand at the back so we can avoid any disruptions.

Who can I bring to the wedding?

Due to space limitations, we kindly ask that only guests specifically named on the invitation attend. If you’re unsure, feel free to reach out to Colin or Zoe!

Is there a dress code?

Yes! We want you to use our wedding as an excuse to wear your finest clothes and look your best! Please check out our Attire Page for more details.

When should I RSVP by and can I change my RSVP?

We kindly ask that you RSVP by June 1st to ensure we can accommodate everyone comfortably. If your plans change after you RSVP, please contact Colin or Zoe directly as soon as possible.

Are phones allowed during the ceremony?

No! We ask that everyone turn off their phones to avoid distractions during the ceremony. We have hired some amazing photographers to capure this special moment for all of us, and we will gladly share the photos after the wedding.

Who should I contact if I have questions on the day of the wedding?

We ask that you reach out to our wedding coordinator, Michelle & Doug Stoermer, or Sheri & Rodney Watson.

Are there hotel blocks available for guests?

Yes, please view our Tevael Page for more details!

Where is the welcome party/ movie night being held?

We will be watching the movie on the lawn at Autocamp. The same location that the reception will be held.

When does the movie night start?

We will be starting the welcome party/movie at 7pm on Friday.

Will food be served at the movie night?

We’ll have light snacks and drinks available.

How do I get into Yosemite

Yosemite is approximately 40 minutes from the venue and is accessible through the Arch Rock Entrance on Highway 140. There is an entrance fee to the park, and July is peak season, so please plan for traffic delays. A park reservation may be required, so we recommend checking Yosemite National Park’s website ahead of time to avoid getting turned away at the gate.
